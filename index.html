FAQ

Good questions.

Are you actually Korean?

Yes. I’m Junseob, a native Korean speaker living in the Seoul metropolitan area (Gyeonggi-do). Korean is my first language, and every Korean line I deliver is written or checked by me.

I don’t speak Korean. How will I know it’s good?

Every Korean line comes with an English back-translation and a short note on tone, so you know exactly what it says and why it’s phrased that way.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a tool for speed and early drafts, like most modern studios. Every design and every word is directed, edited and finalized by me. If you need strictly no-AI work, tell me before ordering and I’ll tell you honestly whether it’s a fit.

What files will I get?

PNG and print-ready PDF for print and social pieces, SVG where useful, and HTML/CSS (or a live link) for websites. I don’t deliver Photoshop (PSD) or Illustrator (AI) source files.

Can you translate my whole website or app?

Landing pages and key screens, yes. For large sites or ongoing app localization, send the scope and I’ll quote it.

How do I pay?

Through Fiverr or Upwork. Your payment is held by the platform until you’re happy with the delivery.
