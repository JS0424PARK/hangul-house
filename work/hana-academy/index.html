Why Hana

하나 means “one.”

One small class, one friendly community, one real conversation at a time. That's how Korean actually sticks.

말

Talk from day one

At least half of every class is speaking. You practise the Korean people use in cafés, group chats and on screen.

8

Small classes, max 8

Enough people to practise with, few enough that your teacher knows your name, and your favourite mistakes.

한

Native Korean teachers

Our teachers grew up in Korea and teach the tone, slang and manners that textbooks skip.

맛

Culture is part of class

Monthly 한식 nights, K-drama watch parties and noraebang (karaoke) Fridays. Fun is how words stay.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Something else on your mind? Add it to your trial booking and we'll answer.

Book a trial
I can't read Hangul at all. Can I still join?
Yes. Hangul in 2 Weeks is made exactly for you. Hangul has 24 basic letters that follow a clear system, so you'll be sounding out simple words by the end of week one.
Is the trial class really free?
Yes. It's one 60-minute class and we never ask for payment details. If you enrol afterwards, we'll help you choose the right level.
Do you offer online classes?
Classes run in person in Koreatown. TOPIK Prep also has a few live online seats, so just ask when you book.
What if I miss a class?
Every class comes with notes and a short practice video. You can also drop into Thursday's Conversation Club to catch up.
Which TOPIK levels does the prep course cover?
TOPIK I (levels 1–2) and TOPIK II up to level 4. After a level check in week one, we split into two groups.
Can parents wait during the kids class?
Of course. There's coffee in the lounge and, yes, Korean snacks too.
